AR Passport Scanner

This scanner uses your camera to detect printed equipment markers and open their digital passport. The video stays on your device — only the marker id (e.g. MARK-001) is sent to the API.

On start, the browser will ask for camera permission. You must Allow it for AR tracking. Camera requires HTTPS (or localhost for local testing).
