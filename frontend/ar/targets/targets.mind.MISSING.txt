PLACEHOLDER — the compiled MindAR database `targets.mind` is NOT checked in.

It must be generated from the marker PNGs with the MindAR image-target
compiler (see README.md in this folder):

  npx mindar-image-target compile -i src/MARK-001.png ... -o targets.mind

Order matters: MARK-001..005 -> targetIndex 0..4.
Without this file, live camera tracking is disabled but manual simulation
(dropdown + ?marker=MARK-001) in index.html keeps working.
